Measure progress beyond keyword positions

Rankings are one view of visibility. Choose a measure to see what it actually explains.

Search impressions and clicks

How often your pages appear, and how often those appearances become visits.

Relevant query visibility

Whether pages are reaching searches connected to the offer, not just any query.

Organic landing-page traffic

Which pages attract visitors through unpaid search.

Tracked enquiries

Whether visitors complete the contact or booking actions you agreed to track.

Enquiry quality

Whether the people contacting you fit the services and the market.

Technical issue resolution

Whether the access and website problems already identified have been addressed.

Which measures apply depends on the engagement and the tracking in place. Tying enquiries to customers or revenue needs reliable CRM data and feedback from sales.
